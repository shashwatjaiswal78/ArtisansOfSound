Frequently asked questions

Can the entertainment fit around our conference sessions?

Yes. Share the agenda and we plan the acts around session timings, breaks and the gala evening.

Can you provide a host in Hindi and English?

Yes. We have anchors who host in Hindi, English and regional languages.

Can you plan for a large gathering?

Tell us the number of attendees and the venue. We confirm which acts and sound set-up suit the room before you decide.

Can we get options before we fix the date?

Yes. Send the city, month and attendance and we reply with options and a quote.
