Corporate Conferences and Summits

Frequently asked questions

Can the music fit around our sessions?

Yes. Share the agenda and we plan the acts around session timings, breaks and the closing evening.

Will the music be too loud for a conference?

No. For arrivals, networking and dinners we suggest instrumental and acoustic acts at a level that lets people talk.

Can you supply a host as well?

Yes. We have anchors who host in Hindi, English and regional languages.
