Before she managed artists, Maneswita was one. That is why Artisans of Sound works the way it does.

As a drummer, percussionist, DJ and composer, she has played 500+ shows, from intimate gigs to large festivals, in clubs, colleges, corporate events and abroad. She knows what an artist needs to give a great show, and what a planner needs to trust that it will happen.

That experience is the reason we are good at this. She knows which questions to ask before a booking, what a soundcheck really takes, how a set should be paced for a crowd and which act truly fits a room. She can tell a reasonable rider from one that will not work on the night.

Highlights

Maneswita has been a guest DJ at one of London's top clubs for Bollywood music. In 2023 she was named one of the Top 33 Women Achievers of India. She has opened for renowned artists such as Indian Ocean, Adnan Sami, Anuv Jain and Parikrama, and has worked on films and ads including Gadar 2, Murder Mubarak and a Hyundai TV commercial. She has also appeared on national television on Doordarshan. In 2016 she won the Toyota Feeling the Street contest and toured Australia, representing India. She is a global Indian ambassador for women drummers, and has been featured in top magazines for female drummers around the world.

What we stand for

For clients

We make booking live music simple and safe, whether it is a wedding, a hotel, a college fest or a corporate night.

  • Clear terms up front: fees, sound, travel and payment are set out before you confirm.
  • Acts chosen with care for your room, your crowd and your budget.
  • A written contract, a replacement act if anyone drops out, and one coordinator until the last set.

For artists

We are a management company founded by an artist, so we do the work we wish someone had done for us.

  • Fair pay, agreed upfront and paid on time.
  • Clear communication: the venue, the timings and the set are confirmed in writing.
  • The logistics are ours, so you can focus on your craft and on the stage.
