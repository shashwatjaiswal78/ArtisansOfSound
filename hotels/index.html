Hotels & Hospitality

Creating memorable experiences through live entertainment

We curate live music and entertainment for hotels, resorts, restaurants, bars and luxury hospitality venues. From intimate acoustic sets to live bands and DJ nights, we help your venue create moments guests remember, on a daily, weekly or monthly programme.

Three ways to work with us

  1. Monthly entertainment partnershipMusicians on a fixed schedule, for example a live acoustic singer on Friday, a jazz band on Saturday and an instrumental duo on Sunday. We manage the artists, the scheduling and the replacements.
  2. Special event entertainmentArtists for New Year's Eve, Christmas, Valentine's Day, Diwali, restaurant anniversaries and special guest nights.
  3. Curated entertainment experiencesA complete evening designed for your venue, not just a musician: Jazz & Dinner Nights, Bollywood Unplugged, Sufi Nights, Sunday Brunch Sessions, Cultural Heritage Evenings or Sunset Sessions.

Frequently asked questions

Can we start with a trial?

Yes. A one-month programme is a sensible way to start. Tell us your venue and the nights you want covered and we propose a calendar.

Who pays for sound equipment?

It depends on the venue and the act. We confirm sound needs for each artist and agree who provides what before the programme starts.

How is a programme priced?

It depends on the number of performances, the acts and your venue. Share the nights you want covered and a budget range, and we send a quote.

Do you work with venues outside Delhi NCR?

Yes. We have artists in many states. Tell us the city and we check who can perform there regularly.

Do you work with hotel groups?

Yes. One relationship can cover several properties and outlets.

Ready to talk?

Send a short brief. We reply with options and one clear quote within 24 hours.
