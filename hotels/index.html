Frequently asked questions

Can we start with a trial?

Yes. A one-month programme is a sensible way to start. Tell us your venue and the nights you want covered and we propose a calendar.

Who pays for sound equipment?

It depends on the venue and the act. We confirm sound needs for each artist and agree who provides what before the programme starts.

How is a programme priced?

It depends on the number of performances, the acts and your venue. Share the nights you want covered and a budget range, and we send a quote.

Do you work with venues outside Delhi NCR?

Yes. We have artists in many states. Tell us the city and we check who can perform there regularly.

Ready to talk?

Send a short brief. We reply with options and one clear quote within 24 hours.
