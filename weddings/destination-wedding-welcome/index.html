Frequently asked questions

How do I choose acts for a destination wedding welcome?

Pick acts that suit the destination: Rajasthani folk for Jaipur and Udaipur, for example. See our destination events page for more.

Can you arrange the other functions of the same wedding?

Yes. We plan the music across the functions together, so the acts suit each night and the budget is kept in one place.

How quickly will I get a quote?

We send a shortlist with videos and one clear quote within 24 hours of a clear brief.

Planning a destination wedding welcome?

Tell us the date, city and venue. We reply with options and one clear quote within 24 hours.
