Frequently asked questions

How do I choose acts for a welcome dinner / family dinner?

Keep it soft: acoustic singers, jazz, piano and violin are all good fits. A ghazal singer suits an older crowd.

Can you arrange the other functions of the same wedding?

Yes. We plan the music across the functions together, so the acts suit each night and the budget is kept in one place.

How quickly will I get a quote?

We send a shortlist with videos and one clear quote within 24 hours of a clear brief.
