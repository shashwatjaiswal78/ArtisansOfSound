Weddings

Entertainment for every wedding function

An Indian wedding is many events, and each one calls for different music. Pick the function you are planning to see the kinds of acts that suit it, and the artists you can book.

Pre-wedding celebrations

Families often book different artists for each of these.

  • Engagement / Ring ceremony (Sagai)Soft, romantic music for the ring ceremony: acoustic singers, violin, flute and live bands.
  • Haldi & Mehendi ceremonyLight, folk-flavoured and rhythmic music for the colourful days before the wedding.
  • Sangeet & Cocktail nightLive bands, singers, DJs and hosts for the big dancing night and the cocktail hour.
  • Pool partyUpbeat DJs, percussion and lounge acts for daytime and sunset by the water.
  • Jaggo / Ladies sangeetPunjabi folk songs, dhol and dancing for the traditional ladies' night.
  • Welcome dinner / Family dinnerAcoustic, jazz, piano and ghazal for guests arriving and dining together.

Wedding-day ceremonies

Separate bookings, even when they happen on the same day.

  • Baraat / Ghodi entryDhol, percussion and folk acts to lead the groom's procession.
  • Varmala / Jaimala ceremonyViolin, flute and soft live singers for the garland exchange.
  • Wedding ceremony / PherasTraditional, devotional and classical music around the sacred rituals.
  • VidaaiGentle flute, violin and folk music for the emotional farewell.

Post-wedding celebrations

The reception and the parties around it.

  • Wedding receptionLive bands, Sufi, jazz, celebrity singers and DJs for the formal evening.
  • After partyDJs, rock and party bands for the late-night celebration.
  • Post-wedding brunch / Farewell lunchAcoustic duos, jazz and piano for a relaxed farewell lunch.

Other functions and celebrations

Ceremonies, special evenings and gatherings around the wedding.

  • Roka ceremonyAcoustic singers and a single instrumentalist for a small family ceremony.
  • Chunni / Chunri ceremonyFolk and devotional singers for the blessing ceremony.
  • Chooda ceremonyPunjabi folk singing and dhol for the pre-wedding ceremony.
  • Mayra / BhaatRajasthani folk and traditional singers for the maternal family's ceremony.
  • Mata ki ChowkiBhajan singers and devotional groups for the evening of prayer.
  • Jagran / JagrataDevotional singers and bhajan groups for an all-night gathering.
  • Qawwali nightQawwali and Sufi groups for a powerful, celebratory evening.
  • Sufi nightSoulful Sufi singers and bands, paired with dinner or cocktails.
  • Ghazal eveningGhazal singers and instrumentalists for a refined, seated evening.
  • Bollywood retro nightClassic Bollywood hits that get every generation singing.
  • Musical eveningLive bands and fusion ensembles for a relaxed evening of music.
  • Bachelor / Bachelorette partyDJs, party bands and electronic acts for the friends' night out.
  • Family get-togetherGentle acoustic, ghazal and jazz acts for both families together.
  • Destination wedding welcomeFolk and cultural acts to welcome guests with local flavour.
