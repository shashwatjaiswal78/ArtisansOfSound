Hiring a Singer for Your Wedding: A Checklist Before You Book

27 October 2026 | 2 min read

Need help choosing a singer? We will shortlist singers with videos to match your events.

Choosing a wedding singer is a personal decision, and it is easier when you follow a few clear steps instead of relying only on a name or a short clip.

1. List the events that need music

Write down every function where you want a singer, such as the mehendi, sangeet, cocktail evening and reception. Each has its own mood, and your choice of singer may differ for each.

2. Decide the mood

Do you want romantic and graceful, or loud and celebratory? A sufi voice and a Punjabi performer create very different evenings. Naming the mood first makes the shortlist much shorter.

3. Think about your guests

Consider the languages your guests speak, the age mix and the music they enjoy. A singer who connects with your guests will always beat one who is only impressive on paper.

4. Watch real performance videos

Ask for full videos of live shows, ideally at weddings or similar events. Studio recordings and edited reels can hide weaknesses that appear on a live stage.

5. Check the practical details

  • How long is the set, and does it include a break?
  • Does the singer bring a band, tracks or instruments, and what sound is needed?
  • Can they take requests or prepare a special song?
  • What are the travel and accommodation needs?

6. Confirm everything in writing

Once you decide, the date, the timing, the fee and the technical needs should all be written into a contract. This protects both you and the artist and avoids confusion on the day.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing only on the basis of a famous name, without checking fit.
  • Booking one singer for every function without thinking about the mood of each.
  • Not asking about the sound needs until the last week.
  • Skipping the written contract.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if a singer is good live?

Ask for full videos of live performances at weddings or similar events. Studio recordings and short edited reels can hide weaknesses.

Should the singer match the couple's taste or the guests' taste?

A balance works best. Choose a singer whose style you love and who can also connect with the languages and tastes of your guests.

Can I meet or speak to the singer before booking?

Tell us what you would like to know about a singer, and we will share their videos and answer your questions before you decide.

What should the contract include?

The date, the timings, the duration of the set, the fee, the payment schedule, the technical needs, travel and stay, and the cancellation terms.

What if I want to change the date?

One free reschedule is available with 15 days' notice. Beyond that, the terms in your contract apply, so discuss changes as early as you can.
