How to Pick a Wedding Singer for Your Sangeet, Reception and More

8 November 2026 | 2 min read

A wedding is not one event but several, and a singer who is perfect for a sangeet may be the wrong choice for a quiet ceremony. Instead of looking for the single best name, look for the best fit for each part of your celebration.

Bollywood singers

These are the most popular choice for sangeets and receptions, because guests know the songs and sing along. A strong Bollywood singer can move between romantic, upbeat and dance numbers and read the room as the evening builds.

Punjabi singers

For high energy celebrations, a Punjabi singer is hard to beat. They bring the bhangra beat, the crowd involvement and the sort of energy that gets every generation on the dance floor.

Sufi and ghazal singers

If you want a softer, more refined evening, such as a cocktail hour or a dinner reception, a sufi or ghazal singer sets a graceful mood and gives guests a chance to listen and talk.

Acoustic singers and duos

A singer with a guitar or keys works beautifully for mehendi, a welcome lunch or a small ceremony. It is warm, personal and needs very little setup.

Devotional singers

For pooja, jaagran or ceremonies with a spiritual tone, a devotional singer sets the right atmosphere and respects the occasion.

What to check before you book

  • Watch full performance videos, not only highlights.
  • Ask for a repertoire that matches your guests' languages and tastes.
  • Confirm the length of the set and the sound the singer needs.
  • Ask whether the singer can take song requests from the family.

Questions to ask any singer

  • Which languages and genres are you most comfortable with?
  • How long will you sing, and do you need breaks?
  • Can you perform a song for the couple or a surprise number?
  • Do you bring a band, tracks or instruments, and what sound do you need?
  • Have you performed at weddings similar to ours?

Frequently asked questions

How many singers do I need for my wedding?

Many weddings use a different singer or style for different functions, such as an acoustic act for the mehendi and a Bollywood or Punjabi singer for the sangeet. One versatile singer can also cover several events.

Can one singer perform at all my functions?

Yes, if their style fits each one. It is simpler to manage, but it limits variety. Discuss the schedule and the fee for multiple functions before you decide.

How long should a wedding singer perform?

Sets usually run between 90 minutes and 3 hours, with one break or non stop, to suit your event. Exact timings are written into the contract.

Do singers bring their own sound system?

Artists bring their instruments. The venue normally provides the PA, monitors, mixer, mics and cables, or we can arrange them on rent.

Can the singer perform our special song?

Often yes. Ask early and share the song, so the artist has time to rehearse it.

When should I book a wedding singer?

As early as your dates are fixed. Peak wedding dates in the winter months are booked well in advance.
