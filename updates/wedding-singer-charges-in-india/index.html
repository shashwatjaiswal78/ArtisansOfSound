Wedding Singer Charges in India: What a Package Includes

24 October 2026 | 3 min read

Wedding singer fees are not standard, because every singer works differently. Instead of chasing a number, it is more useful to understand what a package contains, so that you can compare offers clearly.

What a typical package covers

  • The performance itself, for an agreed duration.
  • The singer's own preparation and repertoire.
  • Where applicable, accompanying musicians or tracks.
  • A soundcheck before the set.

What can change the price

  • The singer's experience and demand. Well known voices are booked far in advance.
  • The set length and number of sets. A single set for a reception differs from sets across several functions.
  • Accompanists. A singer with a live band costs more than a singer with tracks.
  • Customisation. A special song for the couple, extra rehearsal or a surprise performance may be extra.
  • Travel and stay. Outstation artists need travel, accommodation, meals and local transport, which are typically billed at actuals.
  • Sound. The venue usually provides the sound system. If it cannot, it can be arranged on rent.

Questions to ask before you pay

  • What exactly is included in the fee?
  • What is the duration of the set, and can it be extended?
  • What happens if the date or timing changes?
  • What is the cancellation and rescheduling policy?

Comparing offers fairly

Put each offer side by side and tick the items above. A lower figure may leave out travel or a soundcheck, while a higher figure may include a live accompanist and a longer set. Without an item by item comparison, it is easy to choose the wrong one.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Paying a large amount without a written contract.
  • Assuming the fee covers several functions when it covers one.
  • Forgetting that sound and equipment may need to be arranged.
  • Not asking what happens if the timing or date changes.

Frequently asked questions

What is usually included in a wedding singer package?

The performance for an agreed duration, the singer's preparation and repertoire, accompanying musicians or tracks where applicable, and a soundcheck. Check each item in your quote.

What can cost extra?

A second function, extra time, a custom song, additional rehearsal or musicians, and travel and stay for outstation artists. Ask for each to be listed.

How much advance do I pay?

A 50% advance confirms the date and the booking. The balance is paid on the event day after soundcheck, before the act goes on stage.

Is travel included in the quote?

For outstation artists, yes. Our quote covers the fee, duration, band size, travel, hospitality, food and transport.

Is the sound system part of the package?

The venue usually provides the sound. If it cannot, we can arrange it on rent. Artists bring their instruments.

Can I add another function later?

Often yes, subject to the artist's availability. Ask as early as you can, and confirm the change in writing.
