Wedding Band Price in India: What Decides the Cost and How to Budget

30 October 2026 | 3 min read

Want a clear price for your wedding? Ask for an itemised quote covering the fee, duration, travel and sound.

Get a quote on WhatsApp

If you search for a fixed price for a wedding band, you will not find a reliable one. Bands differ widely in size, experience and style, and every wedding has its own requirements. What you can do is understand what drives the cost, so that you can compare quotes properly.

What decides the price

  • The size of the band. A duo is very different from an eight piece band with a horn section and percussion.
  • The reputation and demand for the act. Established bands with a strong track record are booked well in advance.
  • The length of the set. A 90 minute set and a 3 hour set are priced differently.
  • The city and the date. Peak wedding dates in the winter season are in the highest demand.
  • Travel and stay. If the band travels from another city, travel, accommodation, food and local transport are part of the cost.
  • Sound and equipment. If the venue does not provide a suitable sound system, it can be arranged on rent, which adds to the budget.

What a good quote includes

A proper quote should be itemised. It should state the performance fee, the duration, the number of musicians, and what travel, hospitality, food and transport are covered. Ask for the details, so you can compare two quotes on equal terms.

How to plan your budget

  • Decide how important live music is to your wedding, and set aside a share of the budget accordingly.
  • Be flexible on dates if you can. A less crowded date may give you more choices.
  • Choose the right size. A smaller, well chosen act often beats a larger one that does not fit the venue.
  • Book early, because the best acts fill up first.

Common budgeting mistakes

  • Comparing two quotes that include different things, such as one with travel and one without.
  • Forgetting sound and equipment, which can add to the cost if the venue has none.
  • Booking late, when the best acts and dates are already taken.
  • Choosing the largest band for a small space, which wastes budget and hurts the sound.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a fixed price for a wedding band?

No. Bands differ in size, experience, style and demand, and every wedding has different requirements, so price depends on your specific event. The reliable way to know is to request an itemised quote.

Why do quotes from different bands differ so much?

Because they often cover different things. Band size, set length, travel, hospitality and sound can all change what a quote includes. Compare them item by item.

Can I save money by choosing a less busy date?

Sometimes there is more flexibility on dates that are in less demand, but it depends on the act. Ask for options on more than one date if you can.

Are travel costs extra?
