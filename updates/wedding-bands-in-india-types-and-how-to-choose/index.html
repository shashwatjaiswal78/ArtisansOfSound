Wedding Bands in India: Types, Styles and How to Choose One

5 November 2026 | 3 min read

Live bands bring something a playlist cannot: energy that responds to the room. At a wedding, that can turn a good evening into one people talk about for years. The challenge is choosing the right kind of band, because bands differ widely in sound, size and style.

The main types of wedding bands

  • Bollywood bands play film music with a singer or two and a full rhythm section. They are a safe and popular choice for sangeets and receptions.
  • Punjabi and dhol fusion bands add percussion and bhangra energy, ideal for baraat arrivals and dance floors.
  • Western pop and rock cover bands suit a younger crowd and cocktail evenings with English language music.
  • Jazz and lounge bands create an elegant atmosphere for cocktail hours and dinners.
  • Sufi and fusion bands blend traditional sounds with modern arrangements for a distinctive evening.

Match the band to the function

You do not need one band for the whole wedding. Many couples book a softer act for the cocktail hour and a high energy band for the main party. Plan the flow of the evening, then choose acts for each stage.

Consider size and space

A six piece band needs more stage space, power and sound than a duo. Check the dimensions of the stage and the venue's rules on sound timings before you decide.

Ask the right questions

  • How long is the set, and are there breaks?
  • Do they bring their own instruments and sound, or does the venue provide them?
  • Can they learn a special song or play a requested first dance?
  • What are the travel and stay requirements?

A quick technical checklist

  • Stage: measure the space and tell us the size, because a larger band needs more room.
  • Power and sound: confirm what the venue provides, such as PA, monitors, drums, mixer, mics and cables, or ask us to arrange them on rent.
  • Timings: check the venue's sound cut off time and plan the set around it.
  • Soundcheck: leave time before the guests arrive, so the band can check levels without rushing.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing by a single viral clip instead of full performance videos.
  • Booking a band that is too large for the stage.
  • Forgetting the transition from the band to the DJ or to dinner.
  • Not agreeing the song list for key moments such as the entry and the first dance.

Frequently asked questions

How many members should a wedding band have?

It depends on the space and the style. A smaller band suits an intimate venue, while a larger band with percussion and horns suits a big stage. Tell us the venue size and we will recommend a lineup.

How long can a band play at a wedding?

Sets usually run between 90 minutes and 3 hours, with one break or non stop. Longer evenings can be split into two sets.

Does the band bring its own instruments?

Artists bring their instruments. The venue normally provides the PA, monitors, drums, mixer, mics, DJ console and cables, or we arrange them on rent.

Can a band play for the baraat?

Some can, especially Punjabi and dhol fusion acts. Mention the baraat when you ask for a quote, because it affects the setup and the timing.

Can a band and a DJ work together?

Yes, and it is a popular plan. The band performs first and the DJ takes over for the late night dance floor. Plan the handover in advance.

Will an outstation band need travel and stay?

Yes. Outstation artists get road or economy air travel, a four star stay, meals and local cabs, billed at actuals. All of it is stated in the quote.
