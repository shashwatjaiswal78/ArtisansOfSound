Singer, Band or DJ for a Birthday Party? How to Book the Right Act

11 November 2026 | 3 min read

Planning a birthday party? Tell us the guest count and the venue, and we will suggest the right act.

Get a quote on WhatsApp

A birthday party is one of the easiest events to make memorable with the right artist, and one of the easiest to get wrong if you book in a hurry. A few clear decisions up front make the whole process simple.

Start with the guest list and the venue

A gathering of 40 people in a living room needs a very different act from 300 guests in a banquet hall. Tell the artist or the booking company how many people are coming, how big the space is and whether it is indoors or outdoors. This decides the size of the act, the sound setup and the kind of set that will work.

Think about who is in the room

Look at the age group. A party for a 25 year old and a party for a 60th birthday need different music. Think about the language, the genres the guest of honour loves, and whether guests will mostly dance, mostly listen, or do both.

Pick the right type of act

  • A DJ is the best choice when the main goal is dancing for a long stretch.
  • A singer or a live band brings atmosphere and a sense of occasion, and works well when you want a mix of listening and dancing.
  • An anchor keeps the programme moving and handles tributes, games and cake moments.
  • A duo or acoustic act suits smaller, more intimate parties.

Decide the length and the flow

Most parties work well with a set of 60 to 90 minutes, followed by a DJ or a break for dinner. Talk through the order of the evening, including the cake cutting, any speeches and the main dance block, so the artist can plan around it.

Check sound and logistics

Confirm who provides the sound system, microphones and power. Share the venue address and the timing early. If the artist is coming from another city, travel and stay need to be planned.

Book early and confirm in writing

Weekend dates fill up quickly, especially during the winter months. Share your date, city, venue and budget, and we will send videos with a quote within 24 hours. A 50% advance confirms the booking, and a written contract follows with everything you agreed.

A simple flow for the evening

  • Arrival: soft background music while guests settle.
  • First set: 60 to 90 minutes of live music, mixing listening songs with a few dance numbers.
  • Cake and tributes: an anchor or the artist leads the celebration.
  • Dinner: a short break, or gentle music at low volume.
  • Dance floor: a DJ or a second set to finish the night.

Common mistakes

  • Booking an act that is too big for the room, so that the music overpowers conversation.
  • Forgetting to check the venue's sound cut off time, which is usually set by local rules and the venue.
  • Not telling the artist about special moments such as the cake cutting or a surprise.
  • Leaving the booking until the last week, when the best acts are already taken.

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book an artist for a birthday?

Book as early as you can, especially for weekends and the winter months. A few weeks is the minimum for most acts, and more time gives you a wider choice.

How long should the performance be?

Most birthday parties work well with a set of 60 to 90 minutes, followed by a DJ or a break for dinner. Longer events can have two shorter sets.

Do I need to arrange the sound system?

Usually the venue provides the sound, or we can arrange it on rent. Artists bring their instruments. Tell us what the venue has, and we will confirm what is needed.

Can the artist take song requests?

Many can. Share the guest of honour's favourite songs in advance, so the artist can prepare them properly.

Should I choose a singer or a DJ?

A DJ is best if the main aim is non stop dancing. A singer or band suits a party where you want atmosphere and a mix of listening and dancing. Many hosts book a singer first and a DJ later in the evening.
