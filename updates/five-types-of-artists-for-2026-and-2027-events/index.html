Five Types of Artists to Consider for Your 2026 and 2027 Events

15 October 2026 | 3 min read

With the 2026 season in full swing and 2027 around the corner, many hosts are already thinking about their next event. Before you search for individual names, it helps to decide which type of artist suits your plan. Here are five to consider.

1. Live bands

A live band is the choice when you want real musicians, atmosphere and a performance. It suits weddings, galas and large celebrations, and it works best with proper stage space and sound.

2. Singers

A solo singer or a duo brings a more personal touch. It is perfect for sangeets, cocktail evenings, receptions and intimate gatherings, and it needs less space and setup than a full band.

3. DJs

A DJ is the right choice for long hours of dancing and a wide mix of music. Many events pair a DJ with a live element, such as a percussionist or a saxophonist, for added energy.

4. Anchors

A good anchor keeps the evening moving. They welcome guests, introduce performances, run games and tributes, and handle the timing of the programme. At a large event, the anchor is often the person who holds everything together.

5. Celebrity appearances

If your goal is attention and a talking point, a celebrity appearance can deliver it. It is usually shorter than a performance and can be combined with a set if you want both.

How to build your lineup

  • Begin with the mood and the goal of the event.
  • Choose a lead act, and then add supporting acts to cover each part of the evening.
  • Book early, because the best acts and dates fill up first, especially in the winter season.

Planning for 2027

If your event is in the next season, start by fixing the date, city, venue and budget. Then choose a lead act, add supporting acts, and keep a buffer for sound and travel. The winter months are in high demand, so early planning gives you the widest choice.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Booking only by name, without checking how the act fits the venue.
  • Forgetting that the anchor shapes the whole evening.
  • Leaving sound and technical needs to the last minute.
  • Not confirming everything in a written contract.

Frequently asked questions

How many artists should I book for one event?

It depends on the length and the mood of the event. A lead act plus a DJ or an anchor works for many evenings, and larger events may add supporting acts.

When should I start booking for 2027?

As soon as your dates are fixed. The winter months are in high demand, and the best acts fill their calendars early.

Do I need an anchor?

For a large event or a programme with several moments, an anchor keeps everything on time and engaged. For a small gathering, you may not need one.

Can I combine different types of artists?

Yes. A band, a DJ and an anchor together is a common and effective combination.
