Artisans of Sound is a musician-focused management company, founded by an artist, for artists. We bring you the bookings. You focus on the music.

Our vision

A music industry where artists are respected, empowered and valued as the true artisans they are, supported by a system that respects their creativity and rights.

Our mission

To empower musicians, so they can focus on their craft without worrying about the logistical hurdles.

Fair pay

Clear fees agreed before the booking, and payments on time.

Clear communication

The venue, the timings and the set are confirmed in writing, so nothing is left to guess.

Respect

You are treated as the artisan you are, on and off the stage.

How to join

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your name, your act, your city and a link to a video or reel.
  2. We review it and get back to you.
  3. If it is a fit, we add you to the roster and start sending you enquiries.
