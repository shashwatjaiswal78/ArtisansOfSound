About The Legendary Wadalis

The Legendary Wadalis carry forward the music of the Wadali Brothers, the Sufi duo from Guru Ki Wadali, near Amritsar in Punjab, from a family that has sung the messages of Sufi saints for five generations. The duo began as bhajan singers at a temple in Jalandhar and made their Bollywood debut with songs by Gulzar for the film Pinjar.

Puranchand Wadali and his younger brother Pyarelal began performing together as a duo in 1975. Puranchand received the Padma Shri in 2005, and the duo shared the Sangeet Natak Akademi Award. Pyarelal Wadali passed away in 2018. Since then, the elder Wadali performs occasionally with his son Lakhwinder Wadali under The Legendary Wadalis name, keeping the gharana's tradition alive on stage.
