Frequently asked questions

Do your artists travel for destination events?

Many of them do. We confirm who is available for your dates and what travel, stay and local transport involve before we send a quote.

Can we use artists based in the destination city?

Where the city has acts on our roster we show them, and using local artists can reduce travel costs. Tell us the city and we suggest both local and travelling options.

Who pays for travel and accommodation?

It depends on the artist and the event. We list travel, stay and sound separately in the quote so you can see exactly what is included.

How early should we book?

Peak wedding dates in destination cities fill up early, so ask for options as soon as you have dates and a venue.
