Terms at a glance

Clear terms, no surprises

Booking and quote

Share your date, city, venue and budget and you get a quote within 24 hours. A booking is confirmed when the advance is received, and a written contract follows with everything agreed.

Sets

90 minutes to 3 hours, with one break or non-stop, to suit your event. Exact timings are written into the contract.

Fees

Fees depend on the act, the format and the city, so every quote is specific to your event.

Payment

A 50% advance confirms the date and the booking. The balance is paid on the event day after soundcheck, before the act goes on stage.

Sound

Artists bring their instruments. The venue provides PA, monitors, drums, mixer, mics, DJ console and cables, or we arrange them on rent.

Travel and stay

Outstation artists get road or economy air travel, a four-star stay, meals and local cabs, billed at actuals.

Cancellation

The advance is refunded or moved if you cancel 30 days or more out. It is retained from 8 to 29 days out, and the full fee is due inside 7 days. One free reschedule with 15 days' notice.

Music licences

IPRS and PPL music licences are not included in our fees and remain with the venue or the organiser.

Weather and force majeure

Weather, act of god and force majeure terms are set out in full in our written proposal, which we send with every quote.
