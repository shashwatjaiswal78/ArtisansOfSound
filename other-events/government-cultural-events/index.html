Frequently asked questions

Can you work with a department's paperwork requirements?

We provide a quotation, booking confirmation and invoice for each booking. Tell us what documents your department needs and we confirm what we can supply.

Do you have folk and regional traditions on your roster?

Our roster includes Rajasthani folk and fusion, Sufi qawwali, ghazal, classical and devotional acts across several states. Tell us the region and tradition you need.

Can you supply an anchor for an official programme?

Yes. We have anchors who host in Hindi, English and regional languages.

Can you plan multi-day programmes?

Yes. Share the number of days and the programme length and we suggest a mix of acts across the days.
