Frequently asked questions

Can you supply a full line-up for a festival?

Tell us the format, the crowd and the budget. We suggest acts across headliners, supporting bands and DJs, and send one quote.

Do you handle college fests outside Delhi NCR?

Yes. We have artists in many states. Share the city and dates and we check who is available.

Can you help with brand collaborations?

Yes. Send the brief and we suggest artists and performers that suit the brand and the audience, and quote for the activation.

Do you supply sound, stage and lighting?

Many acts bring basic sound, and bigger events need a full production. Tell us the venue and we confirm what each act needs and what is included.
