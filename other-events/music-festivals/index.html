Frequently asked questions

Can you build a full line-up?

Tell us the format, the crowd and the budget. We suggest headliners, supporting bands and DJs across the running order and send one quote.

Do you work with ticketed concerts?

Yes. Share the capacity, venue, date and budget and we suggest acts that suit the audience.

Can you bring international acts?

Where dates, travel and budget allow. See our international bands page for the acts we can approach.

Who provides sound, stage and lighting?

Festivals normally need a full production. Tell us what the venue or your production partner provides and we confirm what each act needs.
