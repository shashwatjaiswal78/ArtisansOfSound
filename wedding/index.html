Wedding artists by city

Find wedding DJs, live bands, singers and anchors for your city. Pick a city to see the acts and get a quote.

  • Wedding artists in DelhiDelhi and the wider NCR host weddings in farmhouses, banquet halls and hotels right through the winter season, so the best artists book out early for the peak dates.
  • Wedding artists in MumbaiMumbai weddings run from hotel ballrooms and sea-facing venues to lawns and clubs, and guests often expect a polished, well-timed evening.
  • Wedding artists in LucknowLucknow weddings are known for warm hospitality and a love of music, from sufi and ghazal evenings to full-energy sangeets in hotels and lawns.
  • Wedding artists in JaipurJaipur is a favourite for palace, fort and heritage-hotel weddings, where the artist needs to suit a grand setting as well as a dancing crowd.
  • Wedding artists in UdaipurUdaipur is one of India's best-known destination wedding cities, with lakeside palaces and resorts where families travel in for several days of functions.
  • Wedding artists in JodhpurJodhpur weddings are often held in forts and palaces, and Rajasthani folk and fusion acts sit naturally beside Bollywood sets.
  • Wedding artists in ChandigarhChandigarh, Mohali and Panchkula are known for big Punjabi weddings, where dhol, bhangra and Punjabi hits keep the floor full.
  • Wedding artists in GurugramGurugram weddings are usually held in farmhouses, banquet venues and hotels, with large guest lists and long, late-night sangeets.
  • Wedding artists in AhmedabadAhmedabad weddings often bring together a sangeet, a garba night and large family gatherings, so a mix of folk and Bollywood sets works well.
  • Wedding artists in HyderabadHyderabad weddings are held in hotels, convention venues and resorts, and a mix of Hindi, Telugu and English music often suits the crowd.
  • Wedding artists in BengaluruBengaluru weddings are held in resorts, gardens and hotels, with mixed crowds that enjoy Bollywood, English and regional music.
  • Wedding artists in KolkataKolkata weddings blend Bengali traditions with Bollywood evenings, with a long-standing appreciation for live music.
  • Wedding artists in PunePune weddings are held in hotels, resorts and lawns, with a young crowd that enjoys live bands and DJs.
  • Wedding artists in GoaGoa is a popular beach and destination wedding location, where sunset ceremonies and open-air parties call for acts that suit the setting.
