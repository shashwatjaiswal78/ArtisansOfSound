Frequently asked questions

Do you work with planners outside Delhi NCR?

Yes. We have artists in many states and book for weddings across India, including destination weddings. Tell us the city and dates and we check who is available.

Can you handle several functions of one wedding?

Yes. We plan the music across mehendi, haldi, sangeet, cocktail and reception together, so the acts suit each night and the budget is kept in one place.

How quickly can I get options?

We send a shortlist and a quote within 24 hours of a clear brief.

Do you charge planners to ask for options?

No. Asking for a shortlist and a quote is free. You pay only when you confirm a booking.
