Book artists by occasion

Weddings, sangeets, parties, corporate events and festivals. Find the right kind of act for your occasion.

  • WeddingsA wedding has many moments, and each one needs a different kind of act.
  • Sangeet and cocktail nightsThe sangeet is the loudest, most personal night of the wedding.
  • ReceptionsA reception needs music that feels polished and keeps a mixed crowd comfortable, from arrival to the last dance.
  • HaldiHaldi is bright, informal and daytime.
  • MehendiMehendi is relaxed and sociable.
  • Engagement and rokaEngagements and rokas call for music that feels warm and celebratory without drowning out the ceremony.
  • BaraatA baraat needs rhythm and spectacle: dhol, a DJ and a crowd that wants to dance in the street.
  • Musical pherasMusical pheras add a personal soundtrack to the ceremony, from soft instrumental pieces to singers who match the rituals.
  • Birthday partiesFrom a milestone birthday dinner to a big party, the right act sets the mood for the guest list.
  • Kids birthday partiesMagicians, DJs and anchors who keep children and parents engaged with games, tricks and music.
  • AnniversariesAn anniversary calls for music with feeling: soft retro songs, a ghazal or a singer who can dedicate a number.
  • Baby showers and godh bharaiBaby showers and godh bharai are warm family occasions, best served by gentle, friendly entertainment.
  • HousewarmingHousewarmings are happiest with music that fits a home: devotional singers for the puja and a singer or trio for the party.
  • Retirement and farewell partiesA farewell or retirement party deserves music with memory: retro songs, a warm singer and a host who keeps things personal.
  • Bachelor and bachelorette partiesHigh-energy DJs, rappers and bands for the last night out, at a club, a villa or a pool.
  • Diwali partiesDiwali parties run from family evenings to corporate celebrations.
  • Garba and dandiya nightsGarba and dandiya nights need rhythm, folk songs and a crowd that keeps moving.
  • Holi partiesHoli parties need loud, colourful, danceable music, often outdoors.
  • Mata ki chowkiDevotional singers and groups for chowkis and jagrans, with bhajans that gather the whole family.
  • Christmas partiesCarol groups, choirs, bands and DJs for Christmas events at hotels, offices and homes.
  • New Year partiesNew Year is the biggest night for live music.
  • College festsCollege fests need big crowds, strong sets and artists who know how to work an audience.
  • Corporate eventsFrom annual dinners to team celebrations, corporate events need acts that are reliable, on time and right for the audience.
  • Product launches and brand eventsLaunches and brand activations need acts that draw a crowd and fit the brand: celebrity guests, DJs, anchors and performers.
  • Annual days and award nightsAnnual days and award nights run on a tight programme with performances, hosting and moments of fun.
  • Restaurant, cafe and hotel nightsLive music brings guests in and keeps them there.
  • Brunch and loungeBrunch and lounge settings call for relaxed live music: soft vocals, strings and chilled sets.
  • Private dinnersPrivate dinners need music that sits with the conversation.
  • House partiesHouse parties work best with a compact setup that fits the space: a DJ, a singer or a small band.
  • Pool and beach partiesPool and beach parties run on summer beats: DJs, percussion and bands that handle open air.
