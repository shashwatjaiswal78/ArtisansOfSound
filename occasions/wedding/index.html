How to plan the music

Map the day first, then match acts to moments: a singer or instrumentalist for the ceremony, a band for the sangeet, a DJ for the late night. Share the dates, venues and guest count, and we will suggest a mix and send videos with a quote.

Frequently asked questions

How early should I book wedding artists?

As soon as your dates are fixed. Popular acts fill their winter calendars early.

Can you plan music for the whole wedding?

Yes. We shortlist, finalise and coordinate acts across the events, with one person to call.

Planning an event?

Tell us the date, city and venue. We check availability and send videos with one clear quote within 24 hours.
