How to plan the music

Keep the setup light and mobile, since many haldis are outdoors. Ask about sound that works in open spaces and mid-day timings.

Frequently asked questions

Is a DJ right for a haldi?

Yes, a DJ with a dhol or percussionist is a popular choice.

Can artists perform outdoors in the daytime?

Yes. Tell us the venue and we will confirm the setup.

Planning an event?
