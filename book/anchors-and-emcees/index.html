Frequently asked questions

Do I need an anchor at my event?

For a large event or a programme with several moments, an anchor keeps things moving. For a small gathering you may not need one.

Can the anchor work alongside the band or DJ?

Yes. A good anchor coordinates with the artists and the production team throughout the evening.
