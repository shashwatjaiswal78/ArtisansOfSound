How to choose

Decide first whether you want a full performance or a short appearance, because they are booked and priced differently. Celebrity artists have fixed touring calendars, so confirm your date early and share the venue, audience size and run of show.

Frequently asked questions

How early should I book a celebrity artist?

As early as your date is fixed. Popular artists tour heavily, and wedding and festive seasons fill up first.

What is the difference between an appearance and a live show?

An appearance is a short presence at your event, while a live show is a full performance with sound, a band and a set list. Our guide on choosing a celebrity guest or a live show covers this in detail.

Planning an event?

Tell us the date, city and venue. We check availability and send videos with one clear quote within 24 hours.
