How to choose

Share the mood of the evening and the crowd. Ask for the song mix, how long the act plays and whether it brings its own sound and backline. A set that blends current Bollywood hits with a few retro favourites suits a mixed family crowd best.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose between a live band and a DJ?

A live band brings energy and a stage moment, while a DJ keeps the floor going for hours. Many weddings book a band for the first part of the night and a DJ after it. Our guide on choosing between a DJ and a live band explains the trade-offs.

Can a band play a custom set for my event?

Often yes. Share the mood, languages and any must-play songs when you enquire and we will confirm what each band can do before you book.
