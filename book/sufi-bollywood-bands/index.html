Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between sufi Bollywood and qawwali?

Sufi Bollywood bands perform contemporary sufi and film songs, while qawwali groups perform the traditional call and response form with harmonium and chorus.

Are these bands suitable for weddings?

Yes, they are popular for sangeets, receptions and cocktail evenings.
