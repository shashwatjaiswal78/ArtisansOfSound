How to choose

Match the act to the audience. Close-up magic suits cocktail hours, stage illusions suit larger rooms, and mentalism works well for a seated crowd. Ask about the set length and any venue needs.

Frequently asked questions

Can magicians perform for children and adults together?

Many acts are built for mixed family audiences. Mention the age mix so the right act is suggested.

Can a magician or mentalist work a cocktail hour?

Often yes. Close-up and walk-around sets suit cocktail hours, while a stage show suits a seated crowd. Tell us the format when you enquire.
