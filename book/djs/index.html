How to choose

Ask what a DJ brings: sound, lights, a console and a back-up. For weddings, mention the family mix and key moments such as entries and baraat so the playlist is planned around them. Share your genre preferences and any songs to avoid.

Yes. A DJ with a percussionist, a singer or a band is a popular combination, and several acts on our roster are built that way.
