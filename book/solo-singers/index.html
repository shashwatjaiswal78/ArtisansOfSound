How to choose

Check the repertoire and language mix, and ask whether the singer performs live with a track, a guitarist or a keyboardist. For musical pheras or a quiet ceremony, ask for a short sample of the softer songs.

Frequently asked questions

Is a solo singer enough for a large wedding?

It works well for the ceremony, cocktail and dinner. For a high-energy sangeet or reception many families add a band or a DJ.

Do solo singers bring their own sound?
